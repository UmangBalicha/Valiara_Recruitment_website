01 // ABOUT VALIARA WHAT IS THE CLUB

Valiara is the Finance Club of IIIT Sri City — a student-run space to learn how money, markets and companies work, and to apply it on real problems. Five teams, open to all branches, no finance background needed.

02 // WHY JOIN WHAT YOU GET
1 Learn finance by doing Read financial statements, value a business and understand markets, through real case studies and fintech projects.
2 Compete & hear from the industry Take part in case competitions, quizzes and trading events, and attend guest sessions with people who work in finance.
3 Lead & belong Own events and teams from year one, and meet people who care about the same things you do.
03 // AFTER YOU APPLY WHAT TO EXPECT

After you apply, there will be a basic interview covering:

1 Basic finance knowledge Only the most basic ideas. Nothing advanced.
2 Management skills How you take responsibility and work with others.
3 Communication skills How clearly you put your ideas across.
